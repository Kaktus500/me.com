TUfast Racing Team

October 2019 — October 2022 Tools: Altium, Eagle, Embedded C, LabView

Project Overview

Three years of intensive engineering and development work with the TUfast racing team, focusing heavily on critical low-voltage systems and embedded software.

Key Contributions

  • Design of the electronics for an emergency braking system (PCB design, embedded C), reducing the time for vehicle startup from several minutes to seconds and adding system monitoring via CAN.
  • Implemented new in-car radio telemetry, allowing access to critical sensor information (e.g. SOC, battery temperature) from a 3x greater distance.
  • Contribution to the layout and integration of the low-voltage wiring harness.
  • Commissioning of the car, including debugging of the assembled low voltage system, first wheel turning, and management during first track days.
  • Active monitoring of vehicle operations and data logging during test sessions and official races.
